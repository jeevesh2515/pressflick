## Platform

web

## Users

Short-form video creators making text match cuts (owner's launch brief and10-creators plan). No verified user interviews or testimonials exist.

## Product Purpose

Pressflick turns a typed phrase and chosen focal word into a locally generated1080p text match-cut video. Success is seeing the real effect, choosing settings, and downloading a playable file.

## Positioning

Free, ad-free, MIT-licensed client-side generator. No signup, tracking or cookies. No uploaded phrase. The app is public; source remains in a private repository until the owner authorizes changing its visibility.

## Operating Context

Browser editing, downloadable MP4/WebM for later editing. WebCodecs supports frame-exact video; real-time fallback has timing limitations. Live at https://pressflick.vercel.app on the existing Vercel project. The earlier Cloudflare plan is superseded; no custom domain has been registered in this work. See docs/DEPLOY.md and docs/reviews/vercel-live-audit.md.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Static HTML/CSS/ES modules, no backend, no build, one vendored MIT muxer. No new runtime dependencies without necessity. All fonts/assets local with licences. Landing and editor are separate pages, confirmed in this chat. Preserve all editor functions, fragment sharing and reduced-motion handling. No publication, purchases, account setup or public repo without explicit go.

## Brand Commitments

Name Pressflick stays. Clean room: no original code, copy, design, assets/name/logo. Owner requests a unique, premium landing with a different colour direction and explicitly invokes Impeccable and Design Taste. Plain factual voice. Existing original geometricP asset can be retained/recoloured. No fabricated social proof or claims about customers.

## Evidence on Hand

Actual five-second video in assets/pressflick-demo.mp4; generated frames/poster and original icon/social artwork. Current test results, independent review findings and unresolved human/device checks are recorded in docs/VERIFICATION.md. No human testimonial or public-use statistics are claimed.

## Product Principles

- Demonstrate the real effect.
- Keep phrases local and make output limits clear.
- Preserve fast access to the editor.
- Support facts with evidence and keep unmet gates visible.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Reduced-motion preview starts paused, visible focus/labels, readable contrast. The owner reports the human launch checklist complete on10November2026; device/version details were not supplied and the checks were not independently witnessed. Do not autoplay flashing media on landing.
