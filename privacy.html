Privacy

Last updated 10 November 2026.

The Pressflick app has no account, cookies, analytics or advertising.

The phrase you type and the video you make stay in your browser. They are not sent to us or to anyone else.

If you copy a share link, the phrase and settings appear after the # symbol. This fragment is not sent to the hosting server. Whoever you send it to can read it. Avoid sharing private text.

These files are hosted by Vercel, which can process standard request information such as IP address and time. See Vercel’s privacy notice. Your phrase is not included in these requests.
