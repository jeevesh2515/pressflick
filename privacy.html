Privacy

Last updated 9 November 2026.

Pressflick collects nothing. There is no account, no cookie, no analytics and no advertising.

The phrase you type and the video you make stay in your browser. They are not sent to us or to anyone else.

If you copy a share link, the phrase and settings are in that link. Whoever you send it to can read them.

The company that hosts these files can see standard request logs, such as IP address and time, as with any website.
