# PRD

## Problem

Creators want the "text match cut" (newspaper clipping) effect for short-form video. The leading free tool makes you watch an ad for every video and every regenerate, caps the phrase at 23 characters, and shows nothing until the ad is over.

## Product

A single page. Type a phrase, pick the word to lock, watch the live preview, download a 1080p video. Free, open source (MIT), no ads, no account, no tracking, no server.

## Users

Short-form creators on TikTok, Reels and Shorts. Editors who want a clip to drop into a timeline.

## Must have (all built)

- Phrase input (80 chars) and word picker
- The effect: new page every cut, word locked in place, highlighter, blur
- 9:16, 1:1, 16:9 at 1080p, no watermark
- MP4 export, WebM fallback
- Sound on every cut: 5 sounds or none
- Cuts per second, zoom, blur controls

## Where it beats the original

| | original | ours |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ads | one per generate and per regenerate | none |
| preview | after the ad | live, as you type |
| phrase length | 23 chars | 80 chars |
| clicks to a video | 3 plus an ad | 2 |
| length, highlighter colour, dark pages | no | yes |
| share a setup | no | link with every setting |
| cookies / consent dialog | yes | none needed |
| source | closed | MIT |

## Not doing

Accounts, a backend, uploads, AI features, a general video editor.

## Success

A first-time visitor downloads a video in under 20 seconds without reading anything.
